WHAT THE BOX?

Everyone is a box. Some boxes are players.
Find them. Cut them open. Don't get cut.

  • Blend in. Decoy boxes stand still, so move only when nobody's looking. Press M in game to let decoys wander (they walk and never sprint).
  • Slash a suspicious box with your box cutter. Whiff or cut a decoy and your cutter takes longer to recover.
  • Power-ups (speed, longer reach, bubble-wrap armour, X-ray specs and more) hide inside some decoys and turn up on the floor. Running over to grab one shows everyone you're a player.
  • Everyone sees every slash. Attacking gives away where you are.
